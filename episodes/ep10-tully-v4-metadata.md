## Facts + sources
1. Bilbies dig deep spiral burrows (up to ~2–3 m) and shelter in them during the day. Australian Museum https://australian.museum/learn/animals/mammals/greater-bilby/ · Save the Bilby Fund https://savethebilbyfund.org.au/about-bilbies/ · Perth Zoo https://perthzoo.wa.gov.au/animal/bilby
2. Their big ears give them excellent hearing and let body heat escape, which keeps them cool. Save the Bilby Fund (above) · Taronga https://www.taronga.org.au/wildlife-and-conservation/animals/detail/greater-bilby · NSW National Parks https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/plants-and-animals/bilby
3. Wild bilbies live only in WA, the NT and small areas of south-west Queensland. DCCEEW https://www.dcceew.gov.au/environment/biodiversity/threatened/action-plan/greater-bilby · Qld Government https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/plants-animals/conservation/threatened-species/featured-projects/greater-bilby
4. They are nocturnal, eat insects, seeds, bulbs, fruit and fungi, and get the water they need from their food, so they don't need to drink. Australian Museum · Qld Government · NSW National Parks (above)
