## Facts used (kid wording → source)
1. "I sleep all day in a cosy tree hollow!" — nocturnal; by day retreats to a hollow log, branch or tree trunk (or even a roof).
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/mammals/common-brushtail-possum/
   - https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/plants-animals/animals/discovering-wildlife/possum/common-brushtail-possum
2. "My bushy tail can grip branches, like an extra hand!" — the tail is prehensile (strong, flexible, curls around branches).
   - https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/species/8449
   - https://www.penguins.org.au/conservation/education/nature-notes/brushtail-and-ringtail-possums/
3. "I live in lots of Australia: the east, the north, the south-west, and Tasmania too!" — found in every mainland state/territory and Tasmania; much of northern, eastern and south-western Australia; only scattered/declined in the arid centre.
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/mammals/common-brushtail-possum/
   - https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/plants-animals/animals/discovering-wildlife/possum/common-brushtail-possum
   - https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/species/8449
   - https://www.iucnredlist.org/species/40585/21952080
4. "Fun fact! At night, I munch on leaves, flowers and fruit!" — comes out after dark to feed on flowers, fruit, buds and leaves.
   - https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/plants-animals/animals/discovering-wildlife/possum/common-brushtail-possum
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/mammals/common-brushtail-possum/
