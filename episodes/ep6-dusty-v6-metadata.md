## Facts used (kid wording → source)
1. "I'm Australia's tallest bird!" — the emu is Australia's tallest native bird, 1.6–1.9 m.
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/birds/emu/
   - https://www.wildlife.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0025/91384/Emu.pdf
2. "I can't fly, but I can run really fast!" — emus are flightless, with three-toed feet and powerful legs, and can run at up to about 50 km/h.
   - https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/animals-and-plants/native-animals/birds/native-animal-facts/emus
   - https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/plants-and-animals/emu
   - https://www.wildlife.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0025/91384/Emu.pdf (says up to 48 km/h)
3. "I live in most of Australia, but not in Tasmania!" — found only in Australia, throughout most of the continent; once lived in Tasmania but were wiped out soon after European settlement (Tasmanian emu listed as Extinct under the EPBC Act, last wild birds about 1865).
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/birds/emu/
   - https://www.environment.gov.au/cgi-bin/sprat/public/publicspecies.pl?taxon_id=64437
4. "Fun fact! Dad emus look after the eggs!" — the female leaves and the male incubates the dark green eggs alone for about 8 weeks (up to 10).
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/birds/emu/
   - https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/plants-and-animals/emu
