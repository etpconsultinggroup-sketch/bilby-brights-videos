## Facts used (kid wording → source)
1. "I'm the biggest kingfisher in the world!" — the laughing kookaburra is the world's largest kingfisher, up to about 46 cm long.
   - https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/animals-and-plants/native-animals/native-animal-facts/birds/laughing-kookaburra
   - https://wildlife.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0030/91389/Laughing-Kookaburra.pdf
2. "My laugh tells other birds, this is my home!" — the famous 'laugh' is a territorial call warning other birds to stay away.
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/birds/laughing-kookaburra/
   - https://wildlife.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0030/91389/Laughing-Kookaburra.pdf
3. "I live in the east of Australia, and in Tasmania and the south-west too!" — found throughout eastern Australia (Cape York to SA's Eyre Peninsula); introduced to Tasmania and the extreme south-west of WA.
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/birds/laughing-kookaburra/
   - https://wildlife.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0030/91389/Laughing-Kookaburra.pdf
4. "Fun fact! I gobble up bugs, lizards and even snakes!" — eats insects, worms, lizards, small snakes, frogs and small mammals.
   - https://wildlife.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0030/91389/Laughing-Kookaburra.pdf
   - https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/plants-and-animals/kookaburra
   - https://australian.museum/learn/animals/birds/laughing-kookaburra/
